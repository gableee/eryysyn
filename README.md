# Eryysyn
### AI-Powered Personal Knowledge & Research Platform

**Eryysyn** is an AI-powered knowledge and research platform designed to help people organize, understand, retrieve, and expand their knowledge through intelligent document processing and AI-assisted research.

Rather than functioning as just another AI chatbot, Eryysyn aims to connect a user's personal knowledge with AI capabilities, enabling more meaningful interactions with their documents, information, and research.

## The Vision

We want to explore how AI can go beyond answering individual questions and become a tool for building and navigating a continuously growing body of knowledge.

Eryysyn aims to make it easier to:

- **Organize Knowledge** — Bring documents and information into structured, searchable workspaces.
- **Understand Information** — Ask questions and receive answers grounded in personal documents.
- **Connect Knowledge** — Retrieve relevant information across multiple documents and sources.
- **Conduct Research** — Combine personal knowledge with external information to produce meaningful research.
- **Build Understanding** — Turn accumulated information into something useful, accessible, and reusable.

## What I'm Building

Eryysyn is being developed incrementally, starting with a reliable knowledge-management foundation and progressing toward an intelligent research system.

The planned capabilities include:

- Secure personal workspaces and document management.
- Document ingestion, extraction, and processing.
- AI-powered conversations with uploaded documents.
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with source citations.
- Reliable information retrieval and answer evaluation.
- External web research and source synthesis.
- Multi-step AI research workflows and report generation.

*These are development goals, not claims that all features are currently implemented.*

## Engineering & Learning Objectives

Eryysyn is also a hands-on AI engineering project where I explore and implement the underlying technologies rather than relying entirely on high-level abstractions.

Key areas of exploration:

- **Software Engineering:** System architecture, API design, database modeling, authentication, authorization, and testing.
- **LLM Engineering:** Tokenization, context management, prompt engineering, streaming, model integration, and inference optimization.
- **RAG Engineering:** Document processing, embeddings, vector search, retrieval strategies, reranking, and grounded generation.
- **AI Evaluation & Reliability:** Retrieval metrics, answer quality, faithfulness, citation correctness, security, and observability.
- **Agentic AI:** Tool calling, workflow orchestration, state management, and multi-step research using LangGraph.
- **Production Engineering & MLOps:** Docker, CI/CD, background processing, monitoring, deployment, and cost optimization.

## Development Roadmap

The project follows a progressive development roadmap:

| Phase | Focus | Target Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Product & Architecture | Product blueprint |
| 1 | Full-Stack Foundation | Secure knowledge workspace |
| 2 | LLM Integration | AI chat assistant |
| 3 | RAG From Scratch | Document Q&A with citations |
| 4 | Evaluation & Reliability | Measured and trustworthy RAG |
| 5 | LangChain | Framework-based RAG comparison |
| 6 | External Web Research | Web-connected research assistant |
| 7 | LangGraph & Agents | Multi-step research agent |
| 8 | Production & MLOps | Production-ready platform |

Each phase builds upon the previous one, emphasizing understanding, implementation, evaluation, and continuous improvement.

## Engineering Philosophy

The goal is not simply to make things work, but to understand why they work and when particular engineering decisions make sense.

My development approach follows four principles:

1. **Understand** — Learn the underlying concepts and fundamentals.
2. **Implement** — Build and experiment with the core functionality.
3. **Evaluate** — Measure results and identify limitations.
4. **Improve** — Refine the system through evidence-based engineering decisions.
