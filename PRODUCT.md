# Product

## Register

product

## Users

College students reviewing their own course material (nursing, dentistry, engineering). They upload lecture PDFs
and slides, then read, ask questions and check answers, often in long sessions before exams, on a laptop at a desk
or on a phone between classes. For subjects like nursing a wrong fact is dangerous, so the student must be able to
verify every answer against the page it came from.

Jobs to be done: read the material, understand it by asking questions, remember it (flashcards later), and keep it
organized (notes, bookmarks later).

## Product Purpose

Eryysyn answers questions only from the student's own documents, with a citation to the exact page and excerpt,
or says plainly that the answer is not in them. When two documents disagree it says so and shows both. Success is
a student who trusts an answer because checking it took one click, not because it sounded confident.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, focused. A quiet study space where the answer and its source are the loudest things on screen.
The assistant is called "Ery". Its voice is plain and exact: it states what the documents say, admits when they
do not say it, and never hypes. Closest in feel to Perplexity (answers with numbered sources you can open),
NotebookLM (chat over your own documents, sources side by side), Notion (a quiet document workspace) and the
familiar ChatGPT chat layout.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI chatbot: purple gradients, sparkles, "Ask me anything!" hype, confident answers with no way to check them.
- Childish study app: mascots, loud colours, streak confetti, gamification.
- Crowded dashboard: every feature visible at once, stat tiles and cards everywhere.
- Cold enterprise tool: grey, dense, intimidating admin-panel feel.

## Design Principles

1. **The source is the product.** Every claim carries its citation, and checking it (document, page, exact excerpt) is one click away. A citation that cannot be checked is worse than none.
2. **Honest about uncertainty.** Refusals, disagreements between documents and what Ery actually searched for are shown plainly, never hidden to look smarter.
3. **Quiet until needed.** One primary action per screen; anything not needed right now can be hidden. Reading comfort for long sessions beats visual flair.
4. **Model output is untrusted text.** Answers are displayed as text with our own citation markers, never as raw HTML, and never decorated to look more authoritative than they are.
5. **Familiar over clever.** Chat looks like the chat apps students already know, so attention goes to the content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: body text contrast at least 4.5:1 in light and dark, every control reachable and visible by keyboard,
touch targets at least 44px on phone, no information carried by colour alone (citations and statuses have text),
proper icons with labels instead of emoji, and a reduced-motion alternative for every animation. For medical
subjects the interface tells the student to check the cited page.
